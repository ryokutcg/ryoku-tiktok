Privacy Policy

Ryoku Carrousels · last updated October 2026

Who we are

Ryoku Carrousels is a private tool used by the team of the TikTok account @ryoku.tcg. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • TikTok basic profile (user.info.basic): your display name and open ID, only to show which account is connected.
  • Upload permission (video.upload): to send carousels you generated to your own TikTok inbox as drafts.

We do not read your videos, messages, followers or any other TikTok data.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the computer of the person who runs the tool, in a local file. They are never sent to us or to any third party.

Carousel images are uploaded to a public web page (GitHub Pages) so that TikTok can download them. They contain only trading card pictures and text, no personal data, and are deleted after being sent.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data. The only services involved are TikTok (to receive the drafts) and GitHub Pages (to host the images temporarily).

Your choices

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token file also disconnects the tool.

Contact

Message @ryoku.tcg on TikTok.

En résumé (français)

L'outil n'accède qu'à ton profil TikTok de base et à l'envoi de brouillons. Les jetons de connexion restent sur ton PC. Les images des carrousels sont hébergées temporairement sur GitHub Pages pour que TikTok les récupère. Rien n'est revendu ni partagé.
