Ryoku Carrousels

A private tool made by the team behind the TikTok account @ryoku.tcg (trading card news: Pokémon and One Piece Card Game).

What it does

  • Generates photo carousels ranking the most valuable cards of a trading card set (for example "Top 10 cards of Chaos Rising"), using public card data.
  • Sends the finished carousel to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft with the Content Posting API (upload mode). The creator then reviews it, picks a sound and publishes it from the TikTok app.

The tool never publishes anything by itself and only acts on the TikTok account of the person who logged in.
